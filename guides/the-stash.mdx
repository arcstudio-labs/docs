Have a piece of of dialogue or a scene that you're not sure about? The Stash is a place for you to discard sections of your script, without deleting them completely. Either bring them back later with drag & drop, or delete them for good when you're sure you want to do so.

### Stashing a piece of text

To stash a section of your script, first select it, then click the stash icon. It will appear in the Stash sidebar on the left.

<video src="/videos/Stash-text.mp4" controls />

### Adding a title to your stash

To help with your organization, you can add a title to your stash entry by clicking on **untitled stash** for the stash entry and entering a title.

<video src="/videos/stash-title.mp4" controls />

### Editing stashed text

To edit the text in the stash, click on it and you will open an editing window.

<video src="/videos/Editing-stash.mp4" controls />

### Putting stashed text back in your script

To place the text in your script, drag and drop it from the Stash sidebar into your script. The purple line will show you where the text will be inserted.

<video src="/videos/Recovering-stash-text.mp4" controls />
