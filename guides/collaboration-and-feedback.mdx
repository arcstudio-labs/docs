Arc Studio is built to make all forms of collaboration and feedback frictionless, including:

- Live collaboration with multiple writers
- Offline collaboration where drafts can be written in private and then shared with co-writers when ready
- Securely sharing your script with others for feedback and comments

<a id="block-5a8be1ffb88046b9a9494d84bd568279" />

# Collaboration

You can invite a collaborator to your script with their email address or via a sharing link. Once they accept the invitation your collaborators will be able to read, edit, add to, and comment on your script.

Each collaborator is assigned a unique color so you can see their live cursor position onscreen and what text they’ve added to the script.

The avatars of all the collaborators are shown in the toolbar to the left of the `+` button. The avatar color corresponds to the color of the collaborator’s cursor and text. If the collaborator has added a profile picture, that picture will be shown as their avatar with a colored outline.

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 1](/images/migrated/f86cc35be72569484ed1150f.jpg)

When the collaborator is live in the script a green “live” light appears on the bottom right of the avatar.

<a id="block-4d8a4c8efcbf4310b99efebc1577e421" />

## Managing collaborators

To invite a collaborator to your script, click the `+` to the right of the avatars in the toolbar above your script to open the Share Script menu.

You will see a list of all the script’s current collaborators. The owner of the document will be labeled “Owner.”

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/c61d869eccdbc785d0a36594.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

<a id="block-e52756420c7043d19918af79594b23f6" />

### **Email invitations**

In the box labeled “Enter names or emails…” type the email address of the collaborator and then click **Invite [email address]**

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 2](/images/migrated/b45ef2b9ca288a9e832c3f24.jpg)

You can also enter their name and select it from the dropdown menu if you’ve collaborated with them before on a script in Arc Studio.

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 3](/images/migrated/41aed30259c3eedfd8a527d3.jpg)

You can add multiple collaborators.

<a id="block-192929be12f68099b8bbd52b0f14d885" />

**Access Level**

1. The default access level is **Can edit**, which means that the collaborators you’ll inviting will be able to make changes to the script, but they won’t be able to add or remove collaborators. You can change the access level to:
   - **Admin** - They’ll be able to edit and add or remove collaborators
   - **Read only** - They will be able to read your script, but not edit it or add or remove collaborators
     ![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 4](/images/migrated/c9a0eaf3af0fb7b734ff5619.jpg)

**Account Status**

You can also choose whether you want to require them to have an Arc Studio account, or whether you want them to be able to access your script as a guest (without signing up for Arc Studio).

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 5](/images/migrated/4cde33e4c23377b05d267d26.jpg)

If you select **Guest** they won’t have to log in or create an account, so you can choose the name that will be associated with their edits and comments in the script.

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 6](/images/migrated/34289bcec54ef906ca71a6ae.jpg)

Click “Send Invite” once you’ve entered all the collaborators and set your desired access level and . They’ll receive an email and a notification on the Desk in the app inviting them to join the script.

<a id="block-e6b029ca032e40e98f5383261c8a2e0c" />

### **Sharing links**

You can also invite collaborators to the script by sending them a sharing link.

Start by clicking “+ Create new link” and change the name of the link.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/ddbf7f6b0249e13fd913d386.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

(You can also change the access level for the link, [like with email invites above.](/guides/collaboration-and-feedback#block-192929be12f68099b8bbd52b0f14d885))

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/d7be19cb3fae381501616ce1.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

Then click “Create link” and you’ll see your newly created sharing link appear.

To share the link, click “Copy link.” The link is now copied to the clipboard. You can paste it and share it with your collaborator.

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 7](/images/migrated/417e7bd311eeef3f851fe7c6.jpg)

To delete the link, click the ellipses “…” and select “Revoke.” New collaborators will no longer be able to join using that link. Existing collaborators who joined using that link have to be removed independently.

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 8](/images/migrated/d70d4f4ce0de535361959e69.jpg)

You can create multiple sharing links to use for different collaborators or groups of collaborators so they can be revoked separately.

<Tip>
  Anyone with that link will be able to add themselves as a collaborator.
</Tip>

**Removing collaborators**

To remove a collaborator, simply click on the ellipses “…” next to their name and select “Revoke.”

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 9](/images/migrated/85a16b993ad0485c31104109.jpg)

If the collaborator has not yet accepted the invitation, select “Remove invitation.”

<a id="block-91a00a77564646189a9df62f17f27557" />

**Collaborator color**

To change a collaborator’s color, click on the ellipses “…” next to their name and select “Change color.” Then click on the new color you’d like to assign them.

**Invites**

When you invite someone to collaborate on a script, they’ll receive an email invitation.

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 10](/images/migrated/c0c22768c9f95720e98e9554.png)

The invitation will also appear in the app on the Desk under “Invites.”

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 11](/images/migrated/991677a0fab7b6fadfd4492f.png)

## Viewing changes by writer

You can view the changes to a draft by the writer who made the changes. To start, make sure tracking changes is activated by toggling on “Changes.”

Then click on “Changes” and select “By writer.”

Now all the text added to the draft will be in the [collaborator color](/guides/collaboration-and-feedback#block-91a00a77564646189a9df62f17f27557) of the writer who made the change, which corresponds to the color of the writer’s avatar in the toolbar.

## Branch Copies

You don’t always want your collaborators to see what you’re writing _while_ you’re writing it. With Arc Studio’s Branch Copies you have a private workspace to work in <u>without affecting the main draft</u>. Once you’re ready to share your work with your collaborators you can share the draft with them.

For more, check out our [Branch & Merge Guide](/guides/branch-and-merge).

## Comments

**Comment mode**

At any given moment we either <u>don’t care</u> about comments (like when we’re writing first drafts or new sections of our scripts) or we <u>really care</u> about comments (like when we’re revising and implementing notes). Ideally comments would be unobtrusive in the first scenario, while in the second scenario they’d be readily apparent and easily accessible.

They’s why comments appear by default as small, subtle bubbles in the margins of your script.

But when you access Comment Mode by clicking on “Comments” in the left sidebar:

- All the comments are shown in the left sidebar
- The portion of the script that was commented on is underlined in yellow
- The avatars of each commenter appear in the right margin so you know who commented.
- If the window is big enough, the text of the comment itself is shown in the right margin as well. (If you don’t see the comment text, try expanding the window and unpin the right sidebar to close it.)
  <video controls src="/videos/migrated/19a6ee057f4c66e35e444276.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

**Comments Sidebar**

All your comments are listed in the Comments sidebar. You can scroll through them using the up and down arrows and choose your viewing options by selecting the ‘Comment view settings’ icon.

Unread comments are indicated with a purple dot.

If you click on the comment in the sidebar, the comment opens in the script.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/e4d3a3460e4be4dca604c99a.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

**Add global comments**

To add a global comment to the script, open the Comments sidebar and click the ‘+’

Or use the keyboard shortcut `Cmd` + `M` (`Ctrl` + `M` on Windows)

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 14](/images/migrated/ec0d862ca2ea226925c42b0c.png)

Type your comment and click ‘Comment’ or hit `Shift` + `Return`

**Add script comments**

To add a script comment, select the portion of the script you’d like to comment on and click the comment bubble in the palette bar, or use the keyboard shortcut `Option` + `Cmd` + `M` (`Alt` + `Ctrl` + `M` on Windows.)

Write your comment and click “Comment” or hit `Shift` + `Return`

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/fce2449a9befd41d40300e9b.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

**Reply to comments**

To reply to a comment, click on the text area beneath the comment. Type your comment and click “Reply” or hit `Shift` + `Return`

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/68a2fd15c4daf0bb415b2953.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

**Resolve comment**

When you’ve finished addressing a comment and would like to resolve it, hover your cursor over the comment and click the check mark. The comment will disappear.

(You will still be able to find it in the Comments sidebar by selecting “Show resolved”)

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 15](/images/migrated/11739fc617f021e159ebfe40.png)

**Edit comment**

To edit a comment, hover your cursor over the comment and select the ellipses “more options” menu. Then click “Edit.”

Once you’re done editing the comment, click “Save.”

![Collaboration & Feedback — illustration 16](/images/migrated/e318d97f6eff7ff8c21d5c00.png)

**Delete comment**

To delete a comment, hover your cursor over the comment and select the ellipses “more options” menu. Then click “Delete.”

**Copy link to comment**

To copy a link to a comment, hover your cursor over the comment and select the ellipses ‘more options’ menu. Then click “Copy link to comment.”

**Tags**

You can add and create tags for your comments. Hover your cursor over a comment and click the “#” then select the colored tag.

To create a new tag, click “New tag +” and write the name of the tag. Click on the color circle to change the color of the tag. Then click “OK”

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/b8a1f6b30632b643ac1dc686.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

In the Comments sidebar you can use the “Comment view settings” menu to see the comments with a specific tag.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/bac85c677176d43e8d2cc61f.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

**Mentions**

To mention a collaborator in a comment or reply type “@” and choose their name from the pop-up menu. They will be notified that you mentioned them in a comment.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/eb04aaed437f1cbd4fbbced1.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

**Commenting on Beats**

You can also comment on beats on the Plot Board or in the Outline view. To comment on a beat, click the comment bubble and leave a comment.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/e57aea5b8bd692bbcb611394.mp4" aria-label={"Collaboration & Feedback"} />

# Script Chat

## Start a chat

To start a new chat, click the `…` icon in the top right and select `Start a chat`. The chat window will appear on the right.

![Open chat](/images/migrated/301b9abb493992829c7cba78.gif)

Type in your message, then hit `Return` or click the `Send` button.

![Sending a chat message](/images/migrated/b23b11171b689db445076ede.gif)

If a message is waiting for you, you’ll see a purple dot on the chat icon. Click on the chat icon to re-open the chat window.

<video controls="true" src="/videos/migrated/370e792298c92e8e6d6f840f.mp4" aria-label="Script Chat" />

<a id="block-270929be12f6805fb3d6d46dbdfca83c" />

## Deleting chat messages

To delete a message you sent, hover over the message and click the trash icon.

Then click “Delete” to confirm.

<video controls="true" src="/videos/migrated/0c8997699b580bed75f6ccc9.mp4" aria-label="Script Chat" />

<a id="block-270929be12f680efb39fc83cea024076" />

## React to messages with emojis

To react to messages with emojis, hover over the message and click the emoji icon. Then select the emoji.

![Emoji reaction to message](/images/migrated/526fa6de07e2e7def7eea4c0.gif)

<a id="block-270929be12f6800195d7f0a78357e3b5" />

## Quote and reply to specific messages

To quote and reply to a specific message, hover over the message and click the reply button. Then write your reply and hit `Return` or click the `Send` button.

![Quote and reply](/images/migrated/b6e9d283c26d66d78b92678b.gif)
