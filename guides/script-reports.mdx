Arc Studio’s **Reports** give you greater insight into your script and aid in the production process.

<a id="block-1d1929be12f6801bab05e89e3dd52ca2" />

### Accessing Reports

To access your reports, click the **Reports** pie chart icon in the top right.

![Script Reports — illustration 1](/images/migrated/1b8b6fa51dc0266bdaa34fbf.jpg)

In the reports window you will see the options for the **Scenes report, Locations report,** and **Cast report**. You can also see your **Characters** breakdown and the **Export** option.

<a id="block-1d1929be12f680cd8fc5c8ab5ff933a4" />

## Cast report

![Script Reports — illustration 2](/images/migrated/39d81d75ddb380615eabdec2.jpg)

The **Cast report** shows you:

- All your characters
- How many lines of dialogue they each have (and the total number of lines in the script)
- How many words they each speak (and the total number of words in the script)
- How many speaking scenes they each have
- How many total scenes they are each in

<a id="block-1d1929be12f680d6b891fd1fad8efd8b" />

## Locations report

![Script Reports — illustration 3](/images/migrated/0fd2c389e90255a992ce9431.jpg)

The **Locations report** shows you

- A list of all your locations, sorted by how many times they occur in your script.
- The page and scene numbers they first occur in your script.

<a id="block-1d1929be12f680e6b390d24883d9bd89" />

## Scenes report

![Script Reports — illustration 4](/images/migrated/62261d9da7a9435ffa9483f3.jpg)

The **Scenes report** shows you a list of all your scenes, where they occur, their length, and the characters who speak in the scene.

<a id="block-1d1929be12f680249f71d8692f936442" />

## Exporting reports

You can export any of the three kinds of reports as a .csv file. First, select the report you want so you’re viewing it. Then click the **Export** button on the top right and save it to your computer.

<a id="block-1d1929be12f680109defe8a994576e61" />

## Characters breakdown

![Script Reports — illustration 5](/images/migrated/f8f3d534a5c9ed25499dc76a.jpg)

The **Characters** breakdown shows you the breakdown of how much each character is speaking via the bar graph. You can change the color for each character by clicking the color dot next to their name.

You can also get a gender breakdown by clicking the gender icon next to each listed character. Choose a category, or add a custom one of your own.

You can then see a gender breakdown in the pie chart by the number of characters of each gender or the number of lines.
