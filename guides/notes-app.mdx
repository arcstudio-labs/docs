## Notes

Arc Studio includes a built-in notes app to organize all your ideas for your script.

You can access your notes in the Notes sidebar on the left. The different kinds of notes are identified by the icon to the left of the note name (see below for more). If you click on a note, it will open on the right. The note is resizable, and you can make it full screen.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/8487d45eee1df1d1e1b68f64.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

<a id="block-18867cab757c46c9b249febb28d2af21" />

### Creating Notes

To create a new note, click the `+` at the top of the notes sidebar. This creates a general note that will be added to your notes list.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/4b6938bb7baae6e60a220a77.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

You can also create a new note by clicking the `+` at the top of any open note.

<a id="block-b2ef52cbe3494b81817d9c00885e92d8" />

### Deleting Notes

To delete a note, click the `…` more options menu at the top of the top of the open note, or to the right of the note name in the notes list. Then select `Delete`.

<a id="block-8b21c5b281ce47399d98a39178ce395e" />

### Pinning Notes

To pin a note to the top of your notes list, click the `…` more options menu at the top of the top of the open note, or to the right of the note name in the notes list. Then select `Pin note`.

<a id="block-ac26335e5ece411c8b983bd1a18ad287" />

### Beat Notes

You can create a note for each Beat of your story. This gives you a place to write more about that beat, including dialogue, jokes, and other things you want to include.

To create a Beat note, click on the note icon on the beat.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/778501c9a81150ee2ba9e552.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

The name of the note will be _Note on ‘[Beat title]’_ and it will be listed in your notes list with the Beat icon (a cube:

![Notes — illustration 1](/images/migrated/6e656aec48cb1ad516e58632.png)

You can access the Beat note by clicking the note icon on the beat, clicking the note in the notes list, or by clicking the note icon next to the beat in the Contents sidebar.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/eb47659a182a83307b74160a.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

<a id="block-b9a5da2e796a4ee182dd55eaab879c1b" />

### README Notes

README notes open up automatically whenever you open the script with the note. You can use this as a reminder of where you left off in your last writing session, or what you plan on writing next.

To create a README note, click the `⋁` in the Notes sidebar next to the `+` and select `Add a README note`

<Tip>
  The title defaults to README, but you can change it.
</Tip>

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/15cc7587f712f885f71d45d0.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

You can designate any note to be the one that opens automatically by clicking the `…` menu at the top of the note and choosing `Open with script`.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/8a86fa3a137c05707b70e7c2.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

<a id="block-e12faa4ad6fd48d4aa84145e1a08d821" />

### Notes on Script Elements (Storylines, Characters, and Locations)

You can also create notes for each Storyline, Character, and Location. To create the note, access your Script Elements in the Elements sidebar on the right. Then click the note icon for the Element you want to create a note on.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/ad6977d3d5c0a83bc2cdd432.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

## Shareable Note Templates

You can create a template from one of your notes to use again or share with others. This can come in handy when using or sharing things like character or plot worksheets.

<a id="block-aee10841b9b84b878b0288db4fecc676" />

### Creating a Note Template

When the note is at the point you want to save it as a template:

1. Click the `…` “more options” menu at the top of the open note (or to the right of the note title in your Notes list in the left sidebar).
2. Select `Save as template`.

<Tip>
  The name of the note will be used as the name of the template.
</Tip>

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/3f601624d2c21c1851943128.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

<a id="block-86e41895f4be48fd876c7b3f1fbcee92" />

### Sharing a Note Template

1. Click the Settings Gear icon.
2. Select `Templates`.
3. Find the note template you want to share and click `Share`. A link has been copied to your clipboard.
4. Paste the link to share it with another Arc Studio user.
5. If the user you’ve shared it with clicks the link, it’ll be added to their account.
   <video controls src="/videos/migrated/d053d37e0d4f9396026f8f28.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

<Tip>
  Here’s the link to the Character Development Questions template shown above! [https://app.arcstudiopro.com/template/Character-Development-Questions-00176c6c22bb](https://app.arcstudiopro.com/template/Character-Development-Questions-00176c6c22bb)
</Tip>

<a id="block-6309dcd53f2d4c41adc0481cb46aca6c" />

### Creating a Note <u>From</u> a Template

1. Open the Notes sidebar on the left
2. Click on the `⋁` (to the right of the `+`) at the top of the notes list to access your note templates.
3. Select the note template you wish to use.

## Organizing Notes With Links

You can organize your notes by using links. This lets you build an interconnected web of all your ideas. You can think of each note kind of like a Wikipedia page. If you hold down `Ctrl` [`Command` on a PC] and click on a link in a note, you go to that note.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/97ac68b3e285cd5d666ea805.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

<a id="block-73a7b1e989f4404ab9b6caf16323d492" />

### Inserting links

To insert a link in a note, type `[[`. This will make a list of all your notes pop up. You can select one of your existing notes from the list, or create a new note with that name.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/c4c0ed047583f5fe118a6715.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

<a id="block-514fa6fa82ae4eccb9ab6c8f780775f8" />

### Backlinks

At the bottom of your note you’ll see “Backlinks.” This is a list of all your other notes that contain a link to the note that’s currently open. Click the `>` to get a preview of that note, or click on it to visit.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/ddc0a6b39415501009918f88.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

<a id="block-dadc5a567c05452da1051499a75c98d7" />

## Turn Into…

<a id="block-eb336b96b1a74745b75e8ba89dcc59d2" />

### Turn into Note

You can turn a highlighted section of a note into a new, linked note.

To start, make sure the section of the note that you’re selecting has a **heading**. This will become the title of the new note. It can be H1, H2, H3, or H4.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/f5c0e032893e672bcd1e9eed.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

Next, highlight the selection of the note that you want to turn into a new note (including the heading). Then use the palette bar at the bottom and select `Turn into Note.`

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/747847296a1ad0de8315695d.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

You’ve created a new note!

You’ve also created a link from the original note to the new note. If you go back to the original note, you’ll see that the heading (which became the title of the new note) is now a link to the new note. Hold down `Command` (`Control` on PC) and click on the link to navigate to it.

<a id="block-c4b36b01f28f4a6893ae782fae7e3969" />

### Turn into Beat

You can turn a highlighted section of a note into a new Beat on the board.

To start, make sure the section of the note that you’re selecting has a **heading**. The heading will become the title of the new Beat. It can be H1, H2, H3, or H4.

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/0b23202242f188eea74949d6.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />

Next, highlight the selection of the note that you want to turn into a Beat. The heading will become the title of the beat. The other text will become the body of a note on the beat. Then use the palette bar at the bottom and select `Turn into Beat.`

<video controls src="/videos/migrated/c7834ef36d8017335a66358d.mp4" aria-label="Notes" />
